# PayGuard AI — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

**Product Name:** PayGuard AI
**Tagline:** Autonomous AI Risk Manager for Digital Payments
**Buildathon:** Razorpay Buildathon
**Track:** Track 2 — AI Risk Manager

PayGuard AI is an intelligent payment-risk monitoring and incident-management platform that continuously observes payment activity, detects abnormal behavior, correlates related signals into incidents, estimates financial exposure, investigates likely causes using AI, and recommends controlled mitigation actions.

The system behaves like a 24/7 payment-risk analyst.

Core loop:

OBSERVE → DETECT → INVESTIGATE → EXPLAIN → RECOMMEND → ACT → MONITOR

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Digital payment systems process large volumes of transactions across:

- UPI
- Cards
- Wallets
- Net banking
- Banks
- Payment providers
- Merchants
- Customers
- Devices

Traditional monitoring may detect that failures have increased, but operations teams still need to determine:

- Why are payments failing?
- Is the issue fraud-related or operational?
- Is a particular bank or payment method degraded?
- Which merchants or customers are affected?
- How serious is the issue?
- How much revenue is at risk?
- What action should be taken?

PayGuard AI is designed to reduce this investigation gap.

## 6. Risk Classification

Every evaluated transaction receives a score from 0 to 100.

| Score | Classification |
|---:|---|
| 0–29 | LOW |
| 30–59 | MEDIUM |
| 60–79 | HIGH |
| 80–100 | CRITICAL |

Possible risk indicators include:

- High transaction amount
- Extreme transaction amount
- High transaction velocity
- Repeated failures
- New device
- Shared device across customers
- New location
- Rapid location change
- Historical customer risk
- Bank failure spike
- Payment-method failure spike
- Merchant failure spike
- Refund anomaly
- Duplicate-payment pattern

---

## 7. Risk Score Architecture

The official score is generated by backend deterministic logic.

Component caps:

- Rule Score: maximum 70
- Anomaly Score: maximum 20
- Contextual Score: maximum 10

Formula:

Final Score = min(100, Rule Score + Anomaly Score + Contextual Score)

The LLM must never generate the official risk score.

---

## 8. Explainability

PayGuard must never display only a number.

Every risk result should include:

- Risk factor
- Score contribution
- Reason
- Threshold
- Observed value
- Historical baseline
- Supporting evidence

Example:

Risk Score: 89 — CRITICAL

+25 Extreme transaction amount
Transaction amount is significantly above historical behavior.

+20 High transaction velocity
Multiple payment attempts occurred inside a short time window.

+10 New device
The customer has not previously used this device.

## 15. AI Safety Requirements

AI must not:

- Invent transaction data
- Invent financial values
- Modify official risk scores
- Modify transaction facts
- Declare fraud without evidence
- Reveal secrets
- Bypass authorization
- Directly execute sensitive financial actions
- Override system policy
- Rewrite historical system decisions

Preferred wording:

- likely
- possible
- suspicious
- unusual
- current evidence suggests

---

## 16. Revenue at Risk

PayGuard should calculate financial exposure for active incidents.

Inputs may include:

- Failed transaction amount
- Affected payment value
- Incident duration
- Failure probability
- Merchant payment volume
- Historical recovery assumptions

Example:

Affected Payment Value: ₹5.10L
Estimated Revenue at Risk: ₹4.28L

The official amount is calculated by backend logic.

AI may explain the number but cannot invent it.
